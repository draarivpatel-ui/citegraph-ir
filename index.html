Hybrid retrieval with an honest refusalv1.0.0

A zero-dependency retrofit benchmark workbench. Okapi BM25 and character n-gram TF-IDF rank a 40-document scientific citation collection, Reciprocal Rank Fusion merges the two runs, and personalized PageRank over the citation graph re-ranks what survives. An abstention head then decides which questions the collection can actually answer, and the calibration, ECE and AUROC of that decision are all reported rather than assumed.

Ranking quality by stage

Lexical is BM25 alone. Dense is character trigram TF-IDF cosine alone. Fused merges the two with Reciprocal Rank Fusion. Re-ranked runs personalized PageRank over the citation graph on top of the fusion. Every stage ranks the whole collection, so recall is measured against the full pool rather than a shortened list, which is why none of the recall columns can reach 1.0000.

Out of distribution abstention

Five questions in the evaluation set have no relevant document anywhere in the collection. The abstention score combines evidence coverage, the agreement of the top two candidates, top confidence and the out of vocabulary ratio of the question against the collection. A question below the threshold is refused rather than answered from a weakly matching document.

The pipeline

Tokenize. Lowercase, strip accents, drop English stop words.
BM25. Okapi over a lexical inverted index, k1 = 1.2, b = 0.75, idf corrected by 0.5 and clamped at zero.
Dense. Character trigram TF-IDF vectors, cosine similarity, boundary padded n-grams.
Fuse. Reciprocal Rank Fusion, k = 60. Only ranks enter, so the two score scales cancel.
Re-rank. Personalized PageRank, damping 0.85, seeded from the fused run, dangling mass redistributed uniformly.
Abstain. Evidence coverage, top two agreement, top confidence and the out of vocabulary ratio, clamped to the unit interval.
