Retraction controls

Retract papers below or by clicking nodes in the support graph. A claim is supported while it can still trace the required number of internally vertex disjoint citation chains down to terminal sources. Retracting a paper removes it and reports exactly which downstream claims collapse and which survive on independent chains.

Checksummed audit export

The report is deterministic JSON: build it twice and the digest is the same. The digest is an unkeyed SHA-256 over the canonical body, so it detects accidental corruption and casual edits. It is not a MAC and not a signature, and it never claims authorship.
